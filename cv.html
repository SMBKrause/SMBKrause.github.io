About

Understanding how microbial diversity shapes ecosystem processes is a central challenge in microbial ecology. My research investigates the ecological drivers of microbial diversity and their consequences for the function and stability of methane cycling. I combine experimentally tractable microbial communities with environmental studies to examine how trace metals, microbial interactions and environmental change influence methane-derived carbon flow.
